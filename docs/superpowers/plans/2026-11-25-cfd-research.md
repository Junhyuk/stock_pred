# CFD chart implementation

Use the CFD dashboard kit’s `DATA_CONTRACT.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `TEST_PLAN.md`, and `AGENTS.md` as the specification. The dashboard displays domestic CFD balance aggregates and the separate FreeSIS notional series. Python, Poppler, SQLite, XKRX, and the existing static dashboard supply the implementation.

## Scope and source decisions

- Use the official [KOFIA CFD disclosure archive](https://www.kofia.or.kr/brd/m_198/list.do) for domestic balances. The downloaded PDFs list the Korean name, a six-character ticker, KOSPI or KOSDAQ, disclosed CFD balance quantity in shares, and amount in units of ₩10,000. The route supplies a posting date. The amount definition’s treatment of margin and the full eligible instrument population remain unverified.
- Use source attribution and keep the raw PDFs, SQLite store, detailed symbol data and experimental results in a private ignored directory. Section 8-9(2) of the [KOFIA regulations](https://law.kofia.or.kr/service/law/lawFullScreenContent.do?historySeq=1450&seq=136) sets attribution and commercial or media use conditions. Keep recurring source requests and public numerical history behind reviewed permission that covers those operations. The public page can show official links and collection status while permission is pending.
- The [FreeSIS sitemap](https://freesis.kofia.or.kr/stats/siteMap.do) exposes distinct gross and net-of-margin CFD notional series through services `STATDRV0100000320` and `STATDRV0100000310`. The [FSC announcement](https://www.fsc.go.kr/no010101/80654) documents the national publication regime. Validate the actual downloadable data, scope, units, availability and usage terms before integrating either series. Display each series according to its own definition.

## 3. Forecast isolation

The weekday workflow refreshes CFD chart data after forecasting and final cache writes, immediately before staging the reviewed Pages files. CFD observations are excluded from training, scoring, and inference for models A, B, and C. The earlier CFD ablation is retained as local research evidence. Its standalone training command has been retired.
