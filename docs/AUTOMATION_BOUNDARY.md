# Research forecast automation boundary

GitHub Actions on `ubuntu-latest` is the runner for the next-session Korea and US research forecast. Cloudflare Workers are not. scikit-learn and LightGBM stay on the GitHub-hosted runner.

One workflow starts Monday–Friday at18:00 Asia/Seoul (`0 9 * * 1-5` UTC), subject to GitHub scheduling delays. Its `postclose` mode collects and freezes actual inputs, restores that immutable bundle, runs next-session forecasts, verifies publication, commits reviewed payloads and deploys Pages.18:00 is the scheduled start, not a fabricated data receipt or publication time. The whole forecast job is capped at25minutes and Pages at5minutes. The separate smoke workflow is manual-only; quick checks run inside the scheduled job.

KST Saturday and Sunday are blocked for all operating modes. Manual validation remains a non-publishing exception. Postclose receipts use the actual completed snapshot cutoff, and each market must still publish before its target session opens. Korea and US eligibility are independent, including holidays and DST. Completion is separated by protocol, market and target session, so an earlier premarket completion does not suppress the new postclose cohort. Failed or partial runs do not claim other markets completed. Legacy manual premarket operations retain their original08:00 boundaries. See [daily operations](DAILY_AUTOMATION.md).

Public Pages files `reliable_index_forecast.json` and `reliable_validation_report.json` omit `candidate_observations`, `observations`, and `calibration_observations`. The full out-of-fold rows stay in the in-memory report and in `archive/{run_id}.json`. Those archive directories are compressed and uploaded as one Actions artifact kept for 14 days. They are not git-committed. Checksums are SHA-256 of the bytes actually written.

`production_weight` stays 0. News fusion alpha stays 0 unless a separate validated model passes its own gate. This job does not approve production and does not change the Ridge market-outlook model.

Price refresh uses Yahoo history and dated Naver KOSPI/KOSDAQ observations for recent completed Korean sessions, including fallback when Yahoo is delayed or rate-limited. Sources and actual receipts remain explicit; an unknown original publication time stays null. Existing index dates and original receipts are not replaced; corrections are separately observed versions. Cross-source conflicts cannot silently supply forecasts. The latest market-close display is independent of model readiness, and an old forecast baseline is explicitly dated. Missing investor flow stays missing. KOSPI100 remains official code1034. KOSDAQ100 stays not ready. The US snapshot is a new immutable dated CSV under `data/processed`. Cached history is incrementally extended; a cache miss requests history from2019-01-01. SQLite, DuckDB, raw CSV, training rows and private caches are never git-committed. This job does not collect Market News Feed.

`after_close_daily_v1` has distinct model, calibration, validation and forward-evaluation state. It retains the existing21-session anchor grid but captures an anchor only during an eligible actual run. Premarket artifacts and retrospective reconstructions cannot be renamed into postclose live evidence. A recalculated research reference may be shown while the strict live model is not ready, with its own retrospective provenance, quality-gate reasons and zero production weight. Historical after-close cutoffs are reconstructed separately from today's actual delay; forward scores start with genuinely observed new publications.

The scheduled job does not collect current news or pass an old event file as current context. X is not scraped.
